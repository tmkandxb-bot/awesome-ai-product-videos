# Case: tiktok-product-video

**Skill:** `tiktok-product-video` (free) — turn a product listing into 3 ready-to-shoot TikTok Shop scripts.

**Input → Output**
- **Input** (`input.md`): the real TikTok Shop listing for the LOVEWE Electric Spin Scrubber ($69.99, sold by Ezify) — price, runtime, handle length, brush-head count, power modes, exactly as the listing states them.
- **Process:** the listing facts were fed into the skill's script formula (Hook 0–3s → Demo 3–20s → Proof 20–28s → CTA 28–35s), once per hook type, so the three variants are three different reasons to stop scrolling — not rewordings.
- **Output** (`output.md`): 3 complete scripts, each with hook line, full voiceover (75–90 words), 5-shot list, caption + 3 hashtags, and cover text — ready to film or hand to an AI video tool.

**What this case proves:** the skill works from a bare listing link's facts alone, invents nothing (no fake certifications, no made-up results), and anchors every CTA to the real listed price.

*Prices/specs were the listing's own on 2026-11-01 and will drift — the example preserves the run, not a live price promise.*
